Privacy Policy for Word Daily

Last updated: October 16, 2026

DailyMint Studio ("we", "our", "us") operates the Word Daily mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Player Name: When you first open the app, you provide a display name.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Game Data: Puzzle completions, scores, streaks, and game statistics.
  • Device Identifier: A unique device ID used to associate your game progress.
  • Premium Status: Subscription purchase status via Google Play Billing.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the game experience
  • To track puzzle progress, scores, and streaks
  • To manage premium subscriptions
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (a cloud database service). Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With Google Play for billing and subscription management
  • When required by law

5. Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information for ad personalization per Google's privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Word Daily is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, we will delete your player profile and game data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted with a revised "Last updated" date.
