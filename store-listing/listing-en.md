## Full description
**Lafz Daily** — a fresh word puzzle every single day, in YOUR language.

🟩 **HOW IT PLAYS**
Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. Green means right letter, right spot.
Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot. Simple, addictive,
and a perfect 3-minute daily brain workout.

🌍 **4 LANGUAGES**
Play in English, اردو (Urdu), العربية (Arabic) or Español (Spanish) —
with full native keyboards and right-to-left support where needed.

🔥 **BUILT FOR DAILY HABIT**
- One new puzzle every day — same word for everyone, everywhere
- Streak counter: don't break the chain!
- Stats: win rate, guess distribution, max streak
- Share your score as emoji squares with friends
- Practice mode: unlimited extra puzzles
- Daily reminder so you never miss a word
- Puzzle archive: replay any past day (Pro)

🎯 **FEATURES**
- 100% free to play, works offline
- Clean, fast, no sign-up needed
- Hints when you're stuck
- Dark-mode friendly design

👑 **LAFZ PRO (optional one-time upgrade)**
Remove ads forever and unlock the full puzzle archive.
