# Privacy Policy — Lafz Daily (working title)

**Last updated:** October 2026

## What we collect
**Almost nothing.** Lafz Daily is designed to respect your privacy:

- **Game data** (streaks, stats, settings) is stored **only on your device**.
  We do not upload it anywhere.
- **No account needed.** We never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- **No location tracking.** The app never accesses your location.

## Advertising
The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may
collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) to show relevant ads
and measure ad performance, as described in Google's Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or opt out of
personalized ads in your Android settings at any time.

Upgrading to Lafz Pro removes all ads.

## In-app purchases
Pro upgrades are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your
payment details.

## Notifications
If you enable the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device.
No data leaves your phone for this feature.

## Children's privacy
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact us at the support email listed on the
Play Store listing.
